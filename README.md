## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes two years of transaction data from a UK-based online retailer to understand:

* Customer spending and purchasing behavior
* Revenue performance and key revenue drivers
* High-value customers and markets
* Cancellation patterns and revenue risk
* Customer value and purchase frequency

The analysis translates these findings into actionable recommendations for **customer retention, revenue protection, and business growth**.

## 🛠 Tools

**Python** · Pandas · NumPy · SciPy · Matplotlib
**SQL / MySQL** · **Tableau** · **Git & GitHub**

## 📊 Key Findings

* Average customer revenue is **£2,916.75**, while the median is **£856.02**, showing highly concentrated customer spending.
* The **top 10 customers generate approximately £2.76M**, or **16.14% of customer-attributed revenue**.
* Purchase frequency has a **strong positive relationship with customer value** (Spearman ρ = 0.859, p < 0.001).
* The **UK generates approximately £14.29M**, representing around **85.5% of completed revenue**.
* **Kitchen & Dining** and **Home Decor & Lighting** generate approximately **£8.47M combined**.
* High-value customers with significant cancellation activity represent an important area of **revenue risk**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Prioritize **high-value repeat customers** using revenue, frequency, and recency.
* Investigate **high-value customers with high cancellation activity**.
* Monitor **cancellation rate and cancelled revenue** as monthly KPIs.
* Protect high-performing product categories and the **UK core market**.
* Explore growth opportunities in established international markets such as **EIRE, the Netherlands, and Germany**.
